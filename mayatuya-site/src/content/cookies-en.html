What are cookies?

A cookie is a small file placed on your browser by a website. Cookies help websites remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and understand how the site is being used. They cannot run programs or deliver viruses.

Some cookies are essential for the site to function. Others are optional and used for analytics or personalisation. We explain each type below.

Cookies we use Essential cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to work. The site cannot function properly without them. They do not require your consent.

  • wordpress_logged_in_* — keeps you logged in to the Aliado portal if you have an account. Session-based.
  • woocommerce_cart_hash, woocommerce_items_in_cart — remembers the contents of your shopping cart. Session-based.
  • woocommerce_session_* — maintains your WooCommerce session during checkout. Session-based.
  • PHPSESSID — standard PHP session cookie. Session-based.
Functional cookies

These cookies remember your preferences to improve your experience. They are set only when you interact with specific features.

  • viewed_cookie_policy — remembers whether you have acknowledged our cookie notice. Expires after 1 year.
Payment processing

When you proceed to checkout, Stripe may set cookies to process your payment securely and to prevent fraud. These are set by Stripe and governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy. We have no access to or control over these cookies.

Analytics

We do not currently use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising cookies. If we introduce analytics in the future, this policy will be updated and your consent will be requested.

How to control cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect how the site functions — in particular, the shopping cart and checkout process may not work correctly without them.
