Destinations

Each city on the Tren Maya route has its own character. We're building a guide to all of them — one verified local network at a time.

On the route
Campeche · Gulf Coast Campeche The walled city. UNESCO heritage, calm streets, and a local life that hasn't been replaced by tourism yet. Available now
Yucatán · Interior Mérida The White City. Colonial grandeur, world-class food, and a cultural scene that never sleeps. Coming soon
Yucatán · East Valladolid The quiet colonial town between Chichén Itzá and the Caribbean. Slow, beautiful, underrated. Coming soon
Quintana Roo · South Bacalar The Lake of Seven Colours. Where the route ends and the water begins. Planned
The Tren Maya connects the Yucatán Peninsula across five states and over 1,500 km of track. Maya Tuya is building a verified local network at each stop — so every traveler on the route finds the right places, not just the first ones.
