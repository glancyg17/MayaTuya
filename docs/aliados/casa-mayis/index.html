About

Casa Mayis is a holistic practice run from Campeche by Mayis — a facilitator, healer, and maker whose work spans aura cleansing, chakra harmonisation, sound therapy, guided meditation, and a range of bodywork traditions including lomi-lomi, shiatsu, and Thai massage. Each session is shaped around the person in front of her. Nothing is off-the-shelf.

Alongside her practice, Mayis produces a collection of handmade ritual objects — candles, herbal and honey infusions, smudging blends, oil preparations, palo santo — each one made with what she calls energetic intention. She also facilitates Cacao Ceremonies using her own ceremonial grade cacao, working with the rhythms of the lunar calendar and creating space for women's circles when the timing calls for it.
