The experience

Campeche's walled city is one of the most intact colonial centres in the Americas. Most people walk through it. This experience is designed so that you actually land inside it — with the stories, the stops, and the people who know it best.

The morning begins outside the walls with the pirate history that built them. It moves through the historic centre at a pace that lets the place breathe — a private chocolate session with one of the city's most serious cacao producers, the UNESCO streets and their 500 years of story, a stop with a local jeweller who makes everything you see by hand, and the oldest church in Campeche told by someone who knows what it survived.

It closes with an organic brunch at the best table in the city. The bill is already paid. The rest of the day is yours. Particularly well suited to first-time visitors to Campeche — every stop is chosen to give you the city's real character, not its tourist surface.
