The context · The Tren Maya route · The change A region in transformation. And a choice.

The Tren Maya connects the Yucatán Peninsula in a way that didn't exist before. Cities that once required four hours by car are now an hour away by train. Campeche, Mérida, Valladolid, Bacalar — all more accessible, more visible, and more within reach of millions of travellers.

That's not neutral. When more people arrive somewhere, the first to benefit are the first to appear. And in the digital economy, that means chains, global platforms, and businesses with budgets to rank — not necessarily the ones that best represent the place.

Maya Tuya exists to change that. We select local businesses with our own editorial judgement. We verify them, present them, and connect conscious travellers — people looking for the real thing — directly to them. No unnecessary intermediaries. No payment to appear. Real standards.

We're not a travel agency. We're not a review platform. We're an editorial network with our own judgement and bilateral commitments to every business we represent.
